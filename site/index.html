Share your screen, not your schedule.

Calendar(s) privacy blur is a Chrome extension that blurs every event title in Google Calendar, Outlook on the web and any other calendar you add. One click on its toolbar button turns it on or off.

Try it: this demo runs the extension's own rules on a sample week.

One click

Blur is on as soon as it is installed. Click the toolbar button to show titles again; an OFF badge reminds you.

Any calendar

Google Calendar and Outlook on the web work out of the box. Add other calendars in settings with your own CSS selector.

Nothing leaves your browser

No network requests, no analytics, no page reading. It only adds and removes a stylesheet. Privacy policy

Settings when you need them

Right-click the toolbar button and choose Options to switch calendars on or off, add another site, or test a rule against an open tab before saving it.

Each site you add asks for its own permission, and you can revoke it from chrome://extensions at any time.
