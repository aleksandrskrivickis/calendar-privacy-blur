Settings

There when you need it.

Right-click the toolbar button and choose Options.

  • Turn each calendar on or off.
  • Add another site with its own selector.
  • Count matches on an open tab before saving.
  • Each new site asks for its own permission.
FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, Meet and others?

Yes. The blur is applied to the calendar page itself, so whatever shares your screen, window or tab shows it blurred.

What exactly is blurred?

The text inside each event: title, time and location. Event colours and the calendar around them stay as they are.

Is anything still visible?

Hover cards and an opened event still show full details; the blur covers the calendar grid. The text is hidden, not deleted, so it protects against onlookers, not against someone at your keyboard.

My calendar was open before I installed it.

Reload that tab once. Tabs opened after installing are covered straight away.

Can I use it with another calendar?

Yes. In settings, add the site’s address and a CSS selector for its events. The extension asks for permission to that site only.
